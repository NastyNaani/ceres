CERES

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

Overview

Ceres is a food barcode scanner. It is designed to keep personal data on your device and does not require an account.

On your device

The following stay on your phone unless you export or share them yourself:

  • Product scan history (barcode, name, score snapshot, image URL when available)
  • Favorites and optional notes
  • App settings (including allergen watch preferences)

Camera

The camera is used only to decode barcodes. Image frames are not uploaded. Only the decoded barcode digits are sent when looking up a product.

Open Food Facts

When you scan or reopen a product, Ceres sends the barcode number to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org) to retrieve product details such as name, brand, Nutri-Score, NOVA group, additives, ingredients, allergens, and related fields.

See the Open Food Facts privacy policy for how they handle requests.

Sharing and exports

If you use Share, Export history, or open a product on Open Food Facts, those actions are initiated by you and may leave your device through the OS share sheet or your browser.

Ads and analytics

This build does not include advertising networks, analytics SDKs, or crash-reporting SDKs.

Children

Ceres is not directed at children under 13. Do not use the app to submit personal information about anyone under 13.
