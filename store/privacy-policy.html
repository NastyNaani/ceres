Ceres privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-09-20

Ceres is a food barcode scanner. It keeps personal data on your device and does not require an account.

On your device

Product history, favorites, notes, and settings stay on your phone.

Open Food Facts

When you scan or reopen a product, the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org) to retrieve product details (name, Nutri-Score, NOVA, ingredients, and related fields). See Open Food Facts privacy.

Camera

The camera is used only to decode barcodes. Image frames are not uploaded.

Ads and analytics

This build does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.
