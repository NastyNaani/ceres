**Category:** Health & Fitness / Food & Drink  

## Full description

Ceres helps you decide at the shelf.

Point your camera at a food barcode. Ceres looks up the product on Open Food Facts and shows a clear verdict — Good, Okay, or Poor — based on Nutri-Score, NOVA processing level, and additives when that data exists.

• Fast barcode scanning  
• Nutri-Score and NOVA at a glance  
• Ingredients, allergens, and additives  
• Private history on your device — no account  
• Same precise dark UI language as a premium scanner  

Data from Open Food Facts. Ceres does not invent a secret scoring algorithm.
