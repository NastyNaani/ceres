# Ceres privacy policy

**Last updated:** 22 September 2026

Public URL (Play Store): https://nastynaani.github.io/ceres/

Ceres is a food barcode scanner. It keeps personal data on your device and does not require an account.

## On your device

- Product history (barcode, name, score snapshot, favorites, optional notes)
- App settings (including allergen watch)

## Camera

The camera is used only to decode barcodes. Image frames are not uploaded.

## Product lookups

When you scan or reopen a product, the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org) to retrieve product details. If that lookup misses or returns little data, Ceres may call its own secure server proxy, which then queries USDA FoodData Central (fdc.nal.usda.gov) for branded nutrition. The USDA API key is kept server-side and is not embedded in the app binary. See [Open Food Facts privacy](https://world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy) and [USDA privacy](https://www.usda.gov/privacy-policy).

## Ads and analytics

This build does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.
